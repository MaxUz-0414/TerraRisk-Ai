Good to know

What counts as one search?

Each time you press Run AI Diagnosis and the analysis succeeds, one search is used. If satellite data cannot be read, nothing is deducted.

Do my searches expire?

No. Credits stack when you buy more and never expire. The Unlimited plan lasts {{ unlimited_days }} days and does not renew automatically; buying it again extends your period.

Is payment real?

This is a course project, so checkout runs in demo mode: your plan is activated but no money or card details are taken.
